I'm Victor, the creator of this site. I make things. Right now those things tend to be electronic, but my inbox is always open to suggestions.

I like figuring out how engineered devices work and learning by doing (aka "experiment until you understand it").
This website is the 6th(?) iteration of my little corner of the internet. 

As of November 2026, I am a strong supporter of the right-to-repair movement, as embodied in Louis Rossmann's [FULU foundation](https://www.fulu.org/).

As the footer probably says, this website is built with the Hugo static-site generator from Markdown source.
